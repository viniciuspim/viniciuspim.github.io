Astray

Evaluating and redesigning a university navigation application based on four years of professional UX design experience.

MSc Project TUD Dublin Dec 2022 UX Design React Native
Original — Dec 2022

Original Product

In December 2022, I completed Astray as my MSc final project at TUD Dublin — a mobile app designed to help students navigate campus, access timetables, and find university services. Built in React Native, it solved a real problem: new students often felt lost during their first weeks, relying on physical maps and staff directions to locate classrooms and facilities.

Role Solo UX Designer & Developer
Tools Figma · Adobe XD · React Native
Timeline Sept – Dec 2022
Platform iOS & Android
Professional Audit — 2026

Retrospective Audit

Four years after graduating, I conducted a professional UX audit of my own work. This wasn't about criticizing my past self — it was about demonstrating growth. The exercise revealed significant gaps in my original approach: weak information architecture, inconsistent navigation patterns, limited accessibility, and most importantly, assumption-based design instead of user research.

User Research

The 2022 version was built on assumptions. The 2026 version is built on evidence. Even though Astray is no longer live, I could still conduct user research. I interviewed 12 people — current TUD students, recent graduates, and international students — to understand their real campus navigation challenges.

Methodology

12 semi-structured interviews (20-30 min each) with current students, recent graduates, and international students

Problem Definition

Primary Problem

"First-year students, especially international students, need a faster and more intuitive way to navigate TUD campuses because they lack familiarity with building layouts, room naming conventions, and campus terminology — which causes stress, lateness, and dependency on staff assistance during their first semester."

Secondary Problem

"All students need instant access to their daily timetable and room locations because current PDF-based solutions are slow and difficult to read on mobile devices, leading to wasted time and scheduling confusion."

User Personas

Visual Identity Redesign

The original design used generic UI patterns and stock colors. The redesign aligns with TUD's brand identity while creating a modern, accessible visual language.

Redesigned — 2026

The Redesign

The redesigned Astray prioritizes speed, clarity, and accessibility. Every decision is informed by user research and modern UX principles.

Information Architecture

The new structure groups features by user intent, prioritizing frequent tasks on the home screen.

Reflection

Returning to this project four years later revealed how much I've grown as a designer. The 2022 version shows I could build interfaces. The 2026 version shows I can solve user problems.

Key Lessons Learned

"This project represents more than a redesign — it's evidence of my evolution from a student who could build interfaces to a designer who solves user problems. The 2022 version shows I could use design tools. The 2026 version shows I can apply UX methodology, conduct research, critique my own work, and deliver solutions grounded in user needs. That's the difference between UI execution and UX thinking."
